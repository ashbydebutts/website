## Skin and soft tissue infections (SSTI)
### Oral options:
- clindamycin
- trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole
- doxycycline or minocycline
- linezolid

### IV options:
- vancomycin 
- linezolid 600 mg BID
- daptomycin 4 mg/kg/dose
- telavancin 10 mg/kg/dose
- clindamycin 600 mg IV TID

## Osteomyelitis

Oral options:
- TMP-SMX 4 mg/kg BID + rifampin 600 mg QD
- linezolid 600 mg PO BID
- clindamycin 600 mg PO q8h

IV options:
- vancomycin
- daptomycin 6 mg/kg QD



## Uncomplicated bacteremia or native valve endocarditis
IV options:
- vancomycin
- daptomycin 6 mg/kg QD

## Prosthetic valve endocarditis
IV options:
- vancomycin + rifampin 300 mg PO/IV q8h + gentamicin 1 mg/kg q8h

## Pneumonia
Oral options:
- linezolid 600 mg BID
- clindamycin 600 mg PO TID
IV options:
- vancomycin
- linezolid 600 mg BID
- clindamycin 600 mg TID
