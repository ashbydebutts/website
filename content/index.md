## What is this?
I'm Ashby deButts, an internal medicine resident physician.

During medical school I took notes with a program called [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), which enables linking some of my thoughts together into a web that can sometimes show unexpected connections or trends.

Through the magic of [community-sourced plugins](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) I can take a few of my notes and make them available to anybody who cares to read them, and that's what I've done on this website. Most of the medical school notes have been removed, but there are now some residency notes available. This is in part an effort to learn in public and reinforce my own knowledge by explaining it to myself, but I hope you learn something while you're here too.
## What to expect
- **Mistakes and errors**, hopefully with corrections. I try to be evidence-based and cite sources, but these are my notes and not a highly-vetted resource.
- Shallow-to-deep dives into things like human physiology, the pathophysiology underlying disease, and modern American healthcare.
- Erratic updates.
